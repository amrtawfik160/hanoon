# Adaptive routing is bounded

Each logical controller turn or worker attempt may receive one batched compatible capability-profile revision and one automatic continuation; a further expansion request is denied and surfaced. A job may automatically promote its task recipe at most twice and may never downgrade it. Planning keeps one critique-driven revision, invalid structured output gets one format correction, and review keeps the configured one-to-ten cycle budget (default three). The same normalized mandatory finding may cause at most two remediation attempts before the third occurrence blocks, even when the broader review budget is larger. Two equivalent provider or model failures promote the next attempt by one configured model pool; exhaustion of the `strong` pool blocks. These ceilings trade some recoverability for predictable cost, termination, and owner-visible control.
