# Mandatory capabilities fail closed

A mandatory capability must produce one terminal `passed`, `findings`, `blocked`, or `failed` outcome before its stage can advance. A missing or failed mandatory capability may use one explicitly declared, admitted substitute with equal or stronger protection; otherwise the job blocks, while an optional denial is recorded and may continue. Guard findings carry stable rule identities, and Hanoon derives their disposition from the capability registry: failed checks, `critical` or `high` findings, requirements-contract violations, and registered `must_fix` rules require remediation; registered advisory rules and remaining `medium` or `low` findings are reported without creating a retry loop. This avoids both silently skipping safety work and allowing reviewer prose to manufacture blockers.
